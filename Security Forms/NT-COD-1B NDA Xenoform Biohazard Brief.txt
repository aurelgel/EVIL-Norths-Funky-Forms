[head=2]Identification[/head]

The Changeling preys upon unique genomes and is able to alter it's form to match that of any victim it has [bold]devoured[/bold].

[bold]Devouring[/bold] leaves genetic damage, dismembers limbs, and eviscerates organs from the victim.

[color=#aaaaaa]If you've run across a body with these signs keep a record of the person as a victim to a changeling. [/color]

There are [bold]definitive ways[/bold] to tell changelings apart from crew:
[bold]
• Inhuman abilities[/bold]
[color=#aaaaaa][italic]Arm blades, EMP shrieks, chemical stings, body regeneration, near-invisibility.[/color]
[bold]
• Blood tests[/bold]
[color=#aaaaaa][italic]Blood run through a centrifuge shows self-preservation and explodes out of the vial.[/italic][/color]
[bold]
• Mismatched memory from original[/bold]
[color=#aaaaaa][italic]A Changeling does not inherit the memories of victims, only physical appearance. Consider asking a suspected changeling what you might've done earlier with them in the shift.[/color][/italic]

[head=2]Countermeasures[/head]
[/italic]
Changelings are extremely [bold]self-sufficient[/bold] with their regenerative properties, however they take time to recover, as such they'll tend to prefer [bold]hit and run[/bold] hunting methods.

It also primarily attacks using it's close range [bold]arm blade[/bold]. It is however [bold]able to use weapons and tools[/bold] like any other humanoid, as such it is important to not grant it access to ranged weaponry to deprive it of that advantage.

[bold]Kinetic weapons[/bold] are to be used by security forces.
[color=#aaaaaa][italic]As energy weapons may be unreliable, and ammo limitations prevent easy re-use should the weapons be lost.[/color][/italic]

[bold]Re-Law Silicons[/bold] to enable them to assist against what they see as "Crew". 
[color=#aaaaaa][italic]The station AI in particular can be very useful for coordinating and trapping the Changeling.[/color][/italic]

[bold]Code-Words[/bold] may be established to help identify one another
[color=#aaaaaa][italic]Best used with recent victims to establish a Mismatch in memory between the Crew and Changeling.

Example Call:           "My soul cries out for truest peace"
Example Response: "But flesh trades rest in mindless ease".[/color][/italic]

[head=2]Termination[/head]

Once the Changeling has been killed, it is to be [bold]cuffed immediately[/bold] to prevent it from escaping once it recovers from it's wounds. 

You will be working with [bold]limited time[/bold] as each passing moment the Changeling gathers it's strenght again.

The [bold]body must be fully destroyed[/bold] to prevent it from rising again, as such you'll be likely using the Chapel's crematorium to [bold]burn the body[/bold] in it's entirety.

[color=#aaaaaa][italic]It is also possible to borg a Changeling, aslong as their consciounsess is trasferred from their body[/color][/italic]

A Changeling can also split itself from it's dead body into a lesser form known as a "[bold]head slug[/bold]", in this form it is unable to  do meaningful harm to crew. However it'll be able to seek a new host by occupying a [bold]dead body[/bold].

Destroying the [bold]Head slug[/bold] will permanentely kill the Changeling for good.[/italic]
