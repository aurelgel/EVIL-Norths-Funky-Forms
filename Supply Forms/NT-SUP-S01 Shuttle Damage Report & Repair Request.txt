                           [bold]NANOTRASEN CORPORATION

─────────────────────────────────────────
[bold]THIS NOTICE SERVES AS AN OFFICIAL REPORT TO THE ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT REGARDING DAMAGE SUSTAINED BY A NANOTRASEN-REGISTERED SHUTTLE OPERATED BY THE SUPPLY DEPARTMENT. REPAIR ACTION IS REQUESTED UNDER ENGINEERING DIRECTIVE 22-K.[/bold]
─────────────────────────────────────────
The Supply Department hereby reports that one or more vessels under its operational control have sustained damage affecting function, safety, or flightworthiness.

The following shuttle has been identified as requiring maintenance:
• [color=#1b67a5]Cargo Shuttle[/color]  
• [color=#1b67a5]Salvage Shuttle[/color]

Damage may include, but is not limited to:
• Hull breaches or structural compromise.
• Meteor or debris impacts.
• Burnt wiring, ruptured fuel lines, or shorted APCs.
• Thruster or navigation failure.
• Mechanical obstruction blocking launch or landing.
• Sabotage, tampering, or hostile action.
• "Normal wear and tear" exceeding Nanotrasen acceptable thresholds.

Engineering is requested to dispatch personnel at their earliest
availability to inspect, repair, and certify the shuttle for
continued station service.

This notice is issued in accordance with:
• Nanotrasen Flight Safety Protocol 5-H  
• Repair & Maintenance Regulation 22-K  
• Interdepartmental Shuttle Responsibility Charter 3-A
─────────────────────────────────────────
