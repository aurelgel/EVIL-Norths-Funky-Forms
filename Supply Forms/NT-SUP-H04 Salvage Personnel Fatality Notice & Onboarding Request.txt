                           [bold]NANOTRASEN CORPORATION

─────────────────────────────────────────
[bold]THIS NOTICE SERVES AS AN OFFICIAL REPORT TO THE HEAD OF PERSONNEL REGARDING THE CONFIRMED LOSS OF A SALVAGE TEAM MEMBER DURING NANOTRASEN-APPROVED EVASIVE, EXPLORATORY, OR RETRIEVAL OPERATIONS.[/bold]
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]The Supply Department hereby reports that a member of the Salvage Team has suffered fatal injuries and is considered:[/bold][/head]
• Deceased.
• Unrecoverable or irretrievable.
• Logistically or physically inaccessible for cloning or recovery.

[head=3][bold]This report acknowledges the following operational facts:[/bold][/head]
• The deceased was acting under authorized salvage orders.
• All reasonable efforts to retrieve remains have been exhausted or are deemed unsafe for crew or station operations.
• Salvage Team staffing levels may now fall below acceptable minimums as outlined in Salvage Operations Standard 9‑F.

Under Nanotrasen Employment Directive 3‑E, the Supply Department hereby requests that the Head of Personnel initiate a hiring announcement to restore operational capacity.

The requested announcement is as follows:

[color=#1b67a5]“Attention crew: The supply department is now hiring qualified personnel for the Salvage Team. Interested applicants should report to the Head of Personnel’s office for evaluation and assignment.”[/color]

This request remains valid until staffing needs are met.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[italic][color=#aaaaaa] I, [color=#1b67a5]the undersigned Quartermaster[/color], affirm that the information above is accurate to the best of my knowledge and submitted in compliance with Nanotrasen Employment Directive 3‑E and Salvage Operations Standard 9‑F.   The Head of Personnel assumes responsibility for announcing, screening, and onboarding replacement personnel.[/color][/italic]
